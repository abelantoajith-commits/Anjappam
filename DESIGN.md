## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of shared abundance, sacred hospitality, and quiet dignity. Named after the biblical miracle of the five loaves, the platform connects food donors—restaurants, wedding caterers, neighborhood bakeries, and families—directly with community members and local shelters seeking nourishment.

### Design Persona & Aesthetic
- **Atmosphere:** Deeply rooted in Kerala’s tradition of open-hearted hospitality (*athithya maryada*). It is pastoral, earth-toned, human, and luminous—eschewing clinical NGO aesthetics and corporate fintech stiffness.
- **Tone:** Reverent without preaching; compassionate without condescension. Dignity is paramount: receiving food must feel as seamless, respectful, and joyful as accepting an invitation to an elder’s table.
- **Visual Movement:** Tactile Warm Modernism. Organic, comforting curves, rich culinary earth tones, paper-like matte surfaces, and gentle light that suggests dawn breaking over harvested fields.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from pantry grains, warm brass vessels, clay hearths, and banana leaves.

- **Primary Accent (`#C98A2B` - Wheat Gold):** Represents provision, harvest, and warmth. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, and uplifting interactive elements.
- **Secondary / Urgent (`#7A2E2E` - Muted Burgundy):** A rich terracotta/wine tone reserved for urgent meal claims, expiring food alerts, and essential verification states without creating abrasive alarm.
- **Tertiary / Action (`#5E7B3A` - Olive Leaf):** Natural, nourishing green representing life, fulfillment, successful food claims, and verified drop-offs.
- **Neutral Primary (`#3B2A1A` - Deep Warm Earth):** High-contrast, soft-black substitute ensuring warm readability across headings and critical text.
- **Neutral Muted (`#6B5440` - Roasted Husk):** Secondary narrative text, captions, and structural labels.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E8D9C5` - Chaff):** Low-contrast structural lines that echo textured parchment.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FFF8EE` (Warm Clotted Cream)
  - Surface Level 1: `#FDF4E7` (Polished Ivory)
  - Surface Level 2: `#F5E8D3` (Toasted Grain)

## Typography

The type system balances reverent, literary beauty with crystalline pragmatic clarity.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Brings humanity, tradition, and an unhurried communal grace to page anchors, impact counts, and collection headings. The organic serifs evoke printed parish circulars and classic Indian literary journals.
- **Systematic Body & Actions (`Inter`):** Keeps complex logistics—such as portions available, pickup timeframes, dietary flags, and geolocation tags—effortlessly legible under direct sunlight on handheld devices.
- **Pacing & Optical Balance:** Pairings must maintain clear breathing room. Generous line-heights are mandated across body text to preserve a calm, dignified reading cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on open hospitality and generous visual space. Density must never feel frantic or utilitarian.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`max-width: 1200px`), collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Rhythm & Safe Margin:** Large negative margins allow food listings and community stories to breathe. Rather than cramming data into dense spreadsheets, listings are composed as individual table settings: spacious, distinct, and easy to parse.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (fluid 4-column layout, bottom-sheet navigations, full-bleed touch targets).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (8 columns, cards in 2-column distribution).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, asymmetric layouts featuring narrative spotlights on donors and community pantries).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is rendered through warm, sunlit physical layering rather than cold, neutral shadows.

- **Ambient Sunlit Shadows:** Shadows avoid pure grey/black alpha values. Instead, they use deep umber hues (`rgba(59, 42, 26, 0.06)` to `rgba(59, 42, 26, 0.12)`) diffused across wider radiuses to emulate natural afternoon light.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Elevation is communicated primarily via subtle shifts in surface container color:
  - Base Level (`#FFF8EE`): Page canvas.
  - Raised Level (`#FDF4E7`): Interactive cards, donation trays, and listing modules.
  - Hover / Float Level (`#FFFFFF` with `#5E7B3A`/`#C98A2B` halo): Floating action buttons, active search drawers, and claim confirmations.
- **Tonal Borders:** Every floating surface uses a crisp `1px` subtle border in `#E8D9C5` to define boundaries gently without rigid compartmentalization.

## Shapes

The form language is organic, soft, and hand-molded—reminiscent of leavened bread, polished terracotta, and rounded river stones.

- **Level 3 (Pill-shaped & Curvature):** Base interactive controls (buttons, input fields, filter tags) adopt fully rounded capsule silhouettes (`rounded-full`).
- **Cards and Containers:** Standard content cards feature `24px` (`rounded-2xl`) radii, while primary focal heroes and modal sheets scale to `32px` (`rounded-3xl`).
- **Inner Corner Harmony:** Nested elements inside cards must use proportionally reduced radii (e.g., `12px` to `16px`) to preserve optical coherence.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Capsule pill (`rounded-full`), filled with Wheat Gold (`#C98A2B`), crisp label in Deep Warm Brown (`#3B2A1A`) or White (`#FFFFFF`) with 600 weight. Subtle ambient glow on hover.
- **Urgent Claim Buttons:** Capsule pill filled with Muted Burgundy (`#7A2E2E`), text in Warm Cream (`#FFF8EE`). Used exclusively for time-sensitive, immediate meal rescues.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Capsule pill with transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#C98A2B` or `#E8D9C5`, text in `#3B2A1A`.

### Badges & Filter Chips
- **Capsule Chips:** Pill shape with `8px` vertical and `16px` horizontal padding. Selected state uses Warm Cream (`#FDF4E7`) with `#C98A2B` border.
- **Dietary & Veg/Halal Badges:** Minimalistic rounded tags utilizing Olive Green (`#5E7B3A`) for strictly vegetarian offerings and Roasted Husk (`#6B5440`) for general details.

### Cards (Food Listings & Donor Highlights)
- **Structure:** Built on `#FDF4E7` with a delicate `1px` border of `#E8D9C5` and `rounded-2xl` boundaries.
- **Metadata Display:** Portions remaining highlighted in bold `Playfair Display`, paired with pickup countdowns, approximate distances, and donor names (e.g., "St. Mary's Community Hall", "Malabar Spice Caterers").
- **Card Hover:** Subtle elevation lift (`translate-y: -2px`) accompanied by a warm earth shadow expansion.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Container:** High-contrast rounded pill inputs with an ivory base (`#FFF8EE`), inset border in `#E8D9C5`.
- **Focus State:** Rich Wheat Gold (`#C98A2B`) perimeter ring (`2px`) with zero harsh edge corners. Placeholder text rendered in roasted husk (`#6B5440`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth `6px` rounded square, filled with Olive Green (`#5E7B3A`) when checked.
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular with a warm concentric dot in `#C98A2B`.

### Bespoke Platform Components
- **The "Portion Loaf" Counter:** A visual indicator utilizing five stylized bread/grain pips that visually deplete as meals are claimed.
- **Blessing / Gratitude Note Cards:** Soft, parchment-like message bubbles (`#F5E8D3`) where recipients can leave anonymous, heartfelt notes of thanks for caterers and home donors.
