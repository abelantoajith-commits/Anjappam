A Sacred Covenant

Sharing food is an act of kindness

“Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.”
Matthew 25:40

At Anjappam, we believe that food prepared with love must never know the darkness of a waste bin. Each unused loaf, vessel of curry, or batch of bread carries the sweat of human hands and the divine gift of providence.

Join our silent, holy fellowship of hot meals across Kerala. Whether you hold excess from a family banquet or care for an empty table awaiting nourishment, your place is reserved here.
